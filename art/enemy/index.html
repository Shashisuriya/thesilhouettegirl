I am my own enemy

Freedom meets the voice within.

DIMENSIONS25 × 25 cmTHEMESelf & identity

THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

This painting explores the conflict between being at peace with yourself and constantly judging yourself. The dancing figure represents freedom, self-expression, and the feeling of becoming one with yourself through dance. Beside her, the devil-like figure represents the critical voice within, constantly watching, questioning, and criticizing every move. Together, they show how we can experience joy and self-acceptance while still carrying an inner voice that tells us we are not good enough. The painting reflects the idea that sometimes, our biggest battle is not with the world around us, but with the way we see ourselves.

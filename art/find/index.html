I hope you find me

The quiet wish to be seen.

DIMENSIONS30 × 40 cmTHEMEConnection & endings

THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

This painting is about feeling lost, unseen, and quietly hoping that someone will notice you even when you are hiding away. The girl sitting alone between the tall red trees feels small and almost swallowed by her surroundings, as if she is waiting for someone to come looking for her. The dark green background creates a feeling of being lost in an unfamiliar place, while the red trees feel like barriers keeping her separated from the outside world. She is not necessarily trying to escape; she is simply waiting and hoping that someone cares enough to search for her. The painting captures that quiet feeling of wanting to be found, understood, and remembered without knowing if anyone will actually come.

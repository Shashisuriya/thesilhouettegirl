THE COLLECTION / 03

I was happier in my dreams

A window into the life we imagine.

DIMENSIONS25 × 25 cmTHEMEDreams & memory

THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

This painting is about finding a kind of happiness in your imagination that feels more real and comforting than reality. The girl is sitting alone by the window, looking at the sunset, almost as if she is somewhere far away in her own mind. The bright sun and peaceful water create a beautiful dreamlike world, while her dark silhouette shows the loneliness she feels in the real one. The purple frame makes the scene feel like she is looking into a dream or another version of her life, one where everything feels calmer and happier. It represents the feeling of escaping into your dreams because sometimes the life you imagine feels better than the life you are actually living.

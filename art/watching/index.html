THE COLLECTION / 05

Who are you when no one’s watching?

Many reflections. One unanswered question.

DIMENSIONS25 × 25 cmTHEMESelf & identity

THE STORY BEHIND THE WORK

This painting speaks about the difference between who we are around other people and who we are when we are completely alone. The girl stands in a hallway surrounded by mirrors, but each reflection looks slightly different, representing the different versions of ourselves that we show to the world. The fact that she is standing in the middle, surrounded by all these reflections, suggests that she is trying to figure out which version is actually her. The bright green and blue colours make the scene feel almost unreal, like she is trapped inside her own thoughts. The painting asks a simple but personal question: when there is no one to impress, no expectations to meet, and no one watching, who are you really?

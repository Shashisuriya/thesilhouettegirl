MEET THESILHOUETTEGIRL

Inner worlds.
Made visible.

A silhouette can hold a feeling that a face cannot quite explain.

Working under the name thesilhouettegirl, she explores the emotional spaces between self-acceptance and self-criticism, imagination and reality, holding on and letting go.

Across this collection, a recurring female figure moves through moonlit landscapes, windows, forests and mirrored hallways. These places become a language for experiences that are deeply personal, yet familiar to many of us.

Her portfolio pairs every painting with its story. The image invites you in; the words reveal the emotional question at its heart.

01 / THE FIGURE

A space for yourself

The dark silhouette leaves room for the viewer’s own memories. Without a detailed face, posture and setting carry the emotion.

02 / THE COLOUR

Emotion as atmosphere

Violet skies feel dreamlike. Red trees become barriers. Warm sunsets offer beauty even when the story is about an ending.

03 / THE THRESHOLD

Between two worlds

Windows, edges and mirrors appear as places of pause: between an imagined life and a lived one, between leaving and staying.
